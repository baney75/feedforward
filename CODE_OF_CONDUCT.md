# Code of Conduct

Feedforward is a place to learn AI, computer science and making. People here are at every level, and many are young. Be the kind of person you would want to learn next to.

This applies on the site (comments, chat, live sessions, shared skills, suggestions) and in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions).

## What we ask

- **Be kind and specific.** Say what is wrong and how to fix it. "This loop never ends because `i` isn't updated" helps. "This is dumb" doesn't.
- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest. Ask a question before you assume a motive.
- **Stay on topic.** AI, computer science, GPUs, math, CAD and 3D printing. If it isn't about learning or building those, it belongs somewhere else.
- **No politics or culture war.** Not in comments, chat, skills, suggestions or pull requests. Even a good argument for something is off topic here.
- **Keep it clean.** No slurs, no cursing at people, no sexual content.
- **Credit people.** Say where an idea, a lab or a snippet came from.
- **Welcome beginners.** Everyone started without knowing what a token is.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, threats, doxxing or following someone around the site.
- Hate toward people for who they are.
- Spam, ads, self-promotion with no teaching value, and link dumping.
- Pretending to be someone else, including staff.
- Trying to get around a moderator action, a rate limit or a ban.
- Sharing someone's private information.

## How to report

- **On the site:** use the **Report** button on a comment. Three reports from different people hide a comment until a moderator looks at it. In chat, tell a moderator or open a GitHub issue.
- **On GitHub:** open an [issue](https://github.com/baney75/feedforward/issues/new/choose), or use the Report option on any comment you can see there. If the report is sensitive, say so in a short issue and the owner will move it to a private channel.
- **Security problems** go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not here.

Reports are read by people, kept as private as possible and never held against the person who made them. Don't report in bad faith.

## What moderators may do

Moderators, admins and the owner act to keep the site useful and safe. They may:

- Hide or delete a comment or chat message.
- Remove an avatar.
- Decline or remove a shared skill.
- Cancel a live session.
- Ban an account. A ban ends its sessions and hides its comments.
- Close, edit or lock an issue or pull request on GitHub, or block someone from the repository.

They act on what was said or done, not on who someone is. Most actions are written to a moderation log that staff can read. If you think a decision was wrong, say so in a GitHub issue. The owner has the last word.
